# CC Macro Analysis OS — V1.2 Phase 2 Research Contract

> **Status: approved and frozen (2026-10-04).** This is the authoritative research specification for
> V1.2 Phase 2 (Private Research Diagnostics). Its binding to the repository, and the few places where
> the implementation adapts wording to the existing architecture, are recorded in
> [`v1.2-phase2-binding.md`](v1.2-phase2-binding.md). The executable form of the signal definitions is
> [`data/dictionary/private_signals.yaml`](../../data/dictionary/private_signals.yaml). Changing a
> formula or threshold there requires a new signal version, never an edit in place.

## 1. Objective

Phase 1 solved:

```text
Wind acquisition
→ runtime verification
→ normalization
→ append-only private snapshots
→ as-of safety
→ public/private boundary
```

Phase 2 must solve:

```text
observations
→ deterministic diagnostics
→ research interpretation
→ hypothesis relevance
→ competing-explanation checks
```

Do not turn Phase 2 into a data-expansion project.

The goal is to make the existing private data materially improve the answer to:

> Is China forming a sustainable turning point in nominal growth?

Phase 2 is therefore a **Private Research Diagnostics Layer**, not a dashboard and not a generic macro signal library.

---

## 2. Non-negotiable research rules

### 2.1 Do not rewrite the preregistration

Existing H0–H4 definitions, baseline, amendments, terminal conditions, and the requirement for domestic-demand confirmation remain unchanged.

Phase 2 diagnostics may:

- strengthen or weaken interpretation of H0–H4;
- expose contradictions;
- identify transmission mechanisms;
- change analyst judgment;
- generate Research Updates.

They must NOT retroactively change the existing automatic terminal rules.

Any future decision to promote a Phase 2 diagnostic into a formal confirmer/falsifier must be a new prospective amendment.

### 2.2 Deterministic computation first

All numeric diagnostics must be produced by code.

LLMs may explain:

```text
what changed
why it matters
which hypothesis it affects
what alternative explanation remains
```

LLMs must not calculate the signal values or invent thresholds.

### 2.3 No fake historical point-in-time analysis

For private Wind observations:

```text
available_at = retrieved_at
```

The first Wind snapshot begins the project's own private vintage history.

Historical rows retrieved in a current snapshot may be used to calculate a diagnostic **today**, but must not be treated as evidence that the same information was available historically.

Do not construct historical private backtests before the project has accumulated genuine snapshots through time.

### 2.4 Private means private

All Wind-derived numeric diagnostics remain `private_only` by default. This includes transformed and aggregated values unless redistribution rights are explicitly reviewed.

Do not expose private numeric signals to GitHub, Netlify, Vercel, or the public static site.

Phase 2 does not build a sanitized public overlay.

---

## 3. Signal architecture

Use three layers:

```text
Layer 1 — atomic diagnostics
    ↓
Layer 2 — research-channel assessments
    ↓
Layer 3 — hypothesis relevance / competing explanations
```

Avoid a single opaque "China Macro Score". Do not optimize weights or thresholds against the thesis outcome. Each signal should preserve its own economic interpretation.

Every signal record should contain approximately:

```text
signal_id
signal_version
run_id
as_of
status
numeric_components
input_observation_ids
calculation_method
coverage_status
mapped_hypotheses
research_role
caveats
private_only
```

`research_role` should be one of:

```text
PRIMARY_EVIDENCE
CONFIRMATORY
CONTEXT_ONLY
COMPETING_EXPLANATION
EXPERIMENTAL
```

## 4. Phase 2 diagnostic blocks

Implement four main research blocks.

### Block A — Liquidity / Financial Conditions

#### A1. DR007 Liquidity Trend

Purpose: is interbank liquidity becoming materially easier or tighter?

Input: `DR007`.

Calculate from complete trading observations:

```text
recent_20d_median
previous_20d_median
delta_bp
```

Initial preregistered interpretation band:

```text
delta <= -10 bp  → EASING
-10 < delta < 10 → STABLE
delta >= +10 bp  → TIGHTENING
```

Also retain the continuous value.

Do NOT call this "monetary-policy stance". DR007 measures market liquidity conditions and may reflect many factors.

Research mapping:

```text
H3 Policy impulse: CONFIRMATORY
H2 Domestic demand: no direct confirmation
```

#### A2. Long-Rate Pricing

Input: China 10Y government-bond yield.

Calculate:

```text
20-trading-day change
60-trading-day change
```

Use it as a market-pricing diagnostic. Interpretation must remain cautious:

```text
yield ↑ ≠ automatically stronger growth
yield ↓ ≠ automatically weaker growth
```

Rates can reflect growth expectations, inflation expectations, policy expectations, supply/demand for bonds, and risk preference.

Research role: `CONTEXT_ONLY`. It may corroborate a story but cannot confirm H1–H4 by itself.

---

## 5. Block B — Credit Demand & Credit Composition

Do not treat the four Wind loan subcomponents as total RMB lending. They represent only the covered components.

Because monthly loan flows have strong seasonality, do NOT use naive sequential MoM comparisons as the primary signal. Use rolling same-period comparisons.

### B1. Household Credit Pulse

Inputs: household short-term new RMB loans; household medium/long-term new RMB loans.

For each component calculate:

```text
latest complete 3-month cumulative flow
same 3 calendar months one year earlier
YoY level delta
```

Do not require a percentage growth rate because loan flows may approach or cross zero.

```text
household_credit_breadth = number of the two components with positive YoY level delta

2 → BROADENING
1 → MIXED
0 → WEAKENING
```

Also calculate, when the denominator is valid, `household_ML_share = ML / (ST + ML)` and compare it with the same period one year earlier.

Interpretation:

- ST improvement is consistent with stronger short-duration household borrowing;
- ML improvement may contain housing-related borrowing and other longer-duration demand;
- neither should automatically be labelled "consumption".

Mapping:

```text
H2 Domestic demand: CONFIRMATORY
H0 Non-sustainable explanation: relevant when persistently weak
```

### B2. Corporate Credit Pulse

Apply the same 3-month same-period methodology to corporate short-term and medium/long-term new RMB loans. Calculate `corporate_credit_breadth` and `corporate_ML_share`.

Interpret carefully. Medium/long-term corporate borrowing can be consistent with investment demand and policy transmission, but may also reflect SOE borrowing, infrastructure financing, policy-driven credit supply, or refinancing. Therefore corporate credit must not automatically be classified as private endogenous demand.

Mapping:

```text
H2 Domestic demand: CONFIRMATORY
H3 Policy impulse / transmission: CONFIRMATORY
```

## 6. Block C — Property Pulse

Inputs remain separate: Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Guangzhou transaction area.

Never add the raw city transaction areas together. City definitions differ.

### C1. City-Level Momentum

For each city calculate:

```text
latest 28-day average
previous 28-day average
28d sequential momentum
```

A 28-day window is used to reduce weekday/weekend composition noise.

```text
>= +5% → POSITIVE
<= -5% → NEGATIVE
otherwise → FLAT
```

Retain the continuous value. Require adequate observations before classification. If coverage is insufficient, return `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rather than imputing a signal.

Holiday effects, especially around Chinese New Year and Golden Week, must be explicitly caveated.

### C2. Property Breadth

Calculate `number_positive`, `number_negative`, `median_city_momentum`.

```text
>= 3 cities positive and median > +5%   → BROAD_IMPROVEMENT
<= 1 city positive and median < -5%     → BROAD_WEAKNESS
otherwise                               → MIXED
```

This is explicitly *four-city high-frequency property confirmation*. It is NOT *China national property-sales growth*.

Mapping:

```text
H2 Domestic demand: CONFIRMATORY
H0 Non-sustainable explanation: important counter-check
```

---

## 7. Block D — Market Confirmation

Inputs: CSI 300; China 10Y government-bond yield; DR007.

The purpose is not to let markets determine the macro thesis. The purpose is: does market pricing corroborate the economic mechanism implied by the thesis?

Calculate:

```text
CSI300 20-trading-day return
10Y yield 20-trading-day change
DR007 liquidity trend
```

Build a deterministic classification such as:

```text
REFLATION_CONFIRMATION
LIQUIDITY_DRIVEN_RALLY
WEAK_GROWTH_PRICING
RISK_OFF_OR_MIXED
MIXED
```

Example logic:

```text
equities materially up + long yields materially up + liquidity not materially tightening
→ consistent with reflation confirmation

equities materially up + long yields flat/down + DR007 materially easing
→ consistent with liquidity-driven rally

equities materially down + long yields materially down
→ consistent with weak-growth / disinflation pricing
```

Exact numerical market noise bands should be declared in config and tested, not hidden in code.

Research role: `CONTEXT_ONLY`. A market rally can never satisfy the domestic-demand or price-turn requirement.

## 8. Higher-level research assessments

After the atomic diagnostics, create only a small number of higher-level deterministic assessments.

### R1. Private Domestic-Demand Confirmation

Inputs: Household Credit Pulse, Corporate Credit Pulse, Property Breadth.

Output: `POSITIVE | MIXED | NEGATIVE | INSUFFICIENT_DATA`.

```text
POSITIVE: at least two independent channels positive,
          with neither household credit nor property showing broad weakness
NEGATIVE: at least two channels negative
otherwise: MIXED
```

This is a private confirmation layer for H2. It does NOT replace the existing preregistered domestic-demand terminal condition.

### R2. Policy Transmission Diagnostic

Question: are easier financial conditions being followed by observable credit / activity response?

Uses DR007 liquidity trend, corporate credit pulse, household credit pulse, property pulse.

Classifications: `TRANSMISSION_VISIBLE | EASING_WITHOUT_RESPONSE | NO_CLEAR_EASING | MIXED`.

Do not infer causality from contemporaneous correlation. As genuine private snapshots accumulate, later versions may evaluate sequencing such as liquidity easing at t → credit/property response at t+1 … t+3. Do not backfill such historical sequencing using the first current-vintage snapshot.

Mapping: H3, H2, H0.

### R3. Market Confirmation Diagnostic

Summarize Block D into `CONFIRMS | DOES_NOT_CONFIRM | CONTRADICTS | MIXED`. Research role remains context-only.

### R4. H0 Competing-Explanation Flag

Question: is apparent nominal-growth improvement occurring without corresponding evidence of domestic-demand improvement?

The flag combines existing public hypothesis/evidence states with the new private diagnostics.

```text
price / production / external evidence improves
AND private domestic-demand confirmation != POSITIVE
→ H0_SUPPLY_LED_RISK = ELEVATED

market rallies but household credit + property remain weak
→ FINANCIAL_MARKET_CONFIRMATION_WITHOUT_REAL_DEMAND

corporate credit improves but household/property channels remain weak
→ POLICY/SUPPLY_LED_CREDIT_RISK
```

This should become a major explicit counter-thesis section in private research updates. It must not automatically overturn the thesis unless an existing preregistered falsifier is triggered.

## 10. CPI/PPI consensus

Do NOT implement a historical surprise model in Phase 2 yet. Until the scheduled real-time CPI/PPI validation demonstrates that a snapshot truly represents a pre-release consensus:

```text
Wind consensus → store snapshot → preserve timestamp → EXPERIMENTAL → no scored thesis signal
```

After successful validation, a separate prospective contract may define pre-release snapshot → actual release → surprise → expectation revision. Do not backfill historical surprises from current Wind consensus history.

---

## 11. Signal storage and reproducibility

A tracked contract/config (`data/dictionary/private_signals.yaml`) defines signal_id, version, inputs, window, formula, classification thresholds, minimum coverage, research role, hypothesis mapping and caveats. No licensed values belong in this file.

Actual outputs are stored only under gitignored private paths:

```text
data/private/signals/<run_id>.json
data/private/research/<run_id>/PRIVATE_DIAGNOSTIC_REPORT.md
```

Every result must identify the exact input observation IDs and transform version. Signal definitions are versioned. Changing a formula or threshold creates a new version rather than silently changing historical meaning.

---

## 12. CLI boundary

```bash
python -m cnmacro refresh-private
python -m cnmacro diagnose-private [--snapshot <run_id>]
```

`refresh-private` remains responsible for acquisition correctness. `diagnose-private` consumes an immutable successful snapshot and generates deterministic research diagnostics. Acquisition and research logic are not hidden in one opaque command.

# Private Macro Diagnostic

## Executive diagnostic

## Credit demand
## Property pulse
## Market confirmation
## H0 competing explanations
## H1–H4 relevance

The most important section is *What changed since the previous genuine snapshot?* Only compare snapshots that genuinely existed at their respective retrieval times.

## 14. Explicit exclusions

Not built in Phase 2: dozens of additional Wind metrics; a dashboard; a public Wind-data overlay; a historical Wind backtest; a historical consensus-surprise model; a black-box composite macro score; learned or optimized signal weights; LLM-generated numerical scores; automatic analyst-probability changes; an automated asset-allocation model; national property claims from the four-city sample; new terminal conditions added retrospectively.

---

## 15. Testing requirements

Regression tests prove at minimum: same inputs → identical signals; future private snapshots cannot enter earlier as-of evaluations; city transaction areas are never summed across cities; insufficient observations → INSUFFICIENT_DATA; missing inputs do not silently become neutral signals; threshold boundaries are deterministic; market confirmation cannot confirm H1/H2 terminal conditions; consensus cannot create surprise evidence before validation; derived private signals remain excluded from public builds; signal results preserve exact input provenance; changing signal version does not rewrite older stored results.

Hand-constructed economic cases include:

```text
easier liquidity + stronger corporate credit but weak household credit + weak property
→ policy/supply-led risk, not broad domestic-demand confirmation

strong CPI/PPI evidence but negative domestic-demand confirmation
→ H0 competing explanation elevated

equity rally + falling yields + easier DR007
→ liquidity-driven rally, not macro-thesis confirmation
```

---

## 16. Phase 2 acceptance criteria

Phase 2 is complete when a real local private snapshot can flow through:

```text
public observations + immutable Wind private snapshot
        ↓
deterministic private diagnostics
        ↓
four research blocks
        ↓
H2 private confirmation · H3 transmission diagnostic · market confirmation · H0 competing-explanation flag
        ↓
private research report
```

and the system can answer, reproducibly: What changed? Is domestic demand actually confirming the nominal-growth turn? Is policy transmission visible? Are markets confirming the same mechanism, or merely reacting to liquidity? Is there evidence that the apparent improvement is still supply-led or non-sustainable? Which conclusion is deterministic, which is contextual, and which remains analyst judgment?

Every numeric result must trace to observations. Every classification must trace to a frozen rule. Every Wind-derived output remains private.

## 17. Implementation discipline

1. Inspect the existing metric IDs, evidence objects, H0–H4 definitions and terminal-rule implementation.
2. Bind this contract to the existing architecture rather than creating parallel domain models.
3. Write the exact proposed `private_signals.yaml` schema and signal formulas first.
4. Confirm that no proposed change alters existing preregistration semantics.
5. Then implement.

Do not expand the metric set unless one of the contracted diagnostics is impossible with existing data and the additional metric has a clearly stated research-information-gain justification.
